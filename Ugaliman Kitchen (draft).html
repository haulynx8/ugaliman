The Ugali Man

George Mokuasi left Kenya as a student and found a new home in Dallas, Texas. He loves rugby, and he noticed fans from Kenya missing the food they grew up on.

So he started grilling nyama choma and cooking fresh ugali at rugby events. Someone once threw "ugali man" at him as an insult. He took the name and made it his kitchen.

Every plate is meant to make you feel at home, whether you're Kenyan or tasting it for the first time.
